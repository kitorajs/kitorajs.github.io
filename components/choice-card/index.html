ChoiceCard

Radio cards and checkbox cards: the whole card is the control, with an icon, a title, a price or other meta, a description and the radio or checkbox indicator; arrow keys in a radio group, a tab stop per checkbox card, form posting and Field wiring.

Source: @kitora/ui (the primitive the kits compose)

type="radio" with three columns from sm up: the whole card is the radio, named by ChoiceCardTitle and described by ChoiceCardDescription and ChoiceCardMeta. Tab enters on the checked card, the arrow keys move and select (← and → swap in right to left).

Props

ChoiceCard

One card: the radio or the checkbox of its group.

  • valuerequiredstring

    The value this card stands for.

  • disabledboolean

    Whether this card is disabled.

  • idstring

    The id of the card's hidden input (what a <label for> would target).

Plus every <div> prop.

ChoiceCardDescription

More about the choice under the title, announced with the card.

Plus every <span> prop.

ChoiceCardGroup

The group of cards (see the module comment).

  • typerequired"radio"

    Makes the group a radio group: one card chosen at a time. Makes the group a checkbox group: any number of cards ticked.

  • valuestring

    Controlled: the chosen card's value. Controlled: the ticked cards' values.

  • defaultValuestring

    Uncontrolled: the card chosen at first. Uncontrolled: the cards ticked at first.

  • onValueChange(value: string) => void

    Called with the chosen card's value when the choice changes. Called with the ticked cards' values when a card is ticked or cleared.

  • namestring

    The form field name the cards post under.

  • formstring

    The form the hidden inputs belong to.

  • disabledboolean

    Whether every card in the group is disabled.

  • readOnlyboolean

    Default false

    Whether the cards show their state but cannot be changed.

  • requiredboolean

    Default false

    Radio: one card must be chosen. Checkbox: at least one card must be ticked.

  • columns1 | 2 | 3 | 4

    Default 1

    Cards per row from sm up (1 to 4); one column on phones.

  • orientation"horizontal" | "vertical"

    Written as aria-orientation and data-orientation (the arrow keys move both ways regardless).

Plus every <div> prop.

ChoiceCardIcon

The card's icon, at the start.

Plus every <span> prop.

ChoiceCardIndicator

The radio dot or the checkbox tick at the end of the card, drawn with the controls' own classes. Optional.

Plus every <span> prop.

ChoiceCardMeta

A short fact beside the title (a price, a limit), announced with the card.

Plus every <span> prop.

ChoiceCardTitle

The card's title: its accessible name.

Plus every <span> prop.

Keyboard

RadioGroup

  • ArrowDownArrowRightRadio.RootMoves focus to the next radio and checks it, wrapping from the last to the first (ArrowRight moves to the previous one right to left).
  • ArrowUpArrowLeftRadio.RootMoves focus to the previous radio and checks it, wrapping from the first to the last (ArrowLeft moves to the next one right to left).

Radio

  • SpaceRadio.RootChecks the focused radio (Enter does nothing, so it never submits a form).

Accessibility

  • RadioGroup follows the WAI-ARIA Radio Group pattern: roles radiogroup, radio.
  • Radio follows the WAI-ARIA Radio Group pattern: roles radiogroup, radio.
