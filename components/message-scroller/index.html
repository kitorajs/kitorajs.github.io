MessageScroller

A focusable chat or log viewport that follows the newest content at the live edge, lets go when the reader scrolls up, keeps their place when older rows load, and shows Jump to latest while away.

Source: @kitora/ui (the primitive the kits compose)

Used inChatMessages · ai-chatMessageList · chatThreadPanel · chat

01Streaming

A reply streams in word by word and the view follows it while you are at the bottom. Scroll up and it lets go at once; Jump to latest brings you back and puts focus on the log.

Props

MessageScroller

The root: owns the stick-to-bottom engine; carries data-away and data-following.

  • autoScrollboolean

    Default true

    Follow the newest content while the reader is at the live edge. False never follows on its own.

  • thresholdnumber

    Default 48

    Px from the bottom that still counts as at the live edge.

  • followingboolean

    Whether the view follows the newest content (controlled, with onFollowingChange).

  • defaultFollowingboolean

    Default true, or false with `defaultPosition="start"`

    Whether the view follows at first (uncontrolled).

  • onFollowingChange(following: boolean) => void

    Called when the reader (or toBottom, stick, scrollToItem) starts or stops following.

  • preserveOnPrependboolean

    Default true

    Keep the first visible row in place when older rows are inserted above it.

  • defaultPosition"end" | "start"

    Default "end"

    Where the log opens: at the newest content (end) or at the top (start).

  • itemAttributestring

    Default "data-message-id"

    The attribute that marks a row and holds its id.

Plus every <div> prop.

MessageScrollerContent

The element inside the viewport that holds the rows; its size changes are what the scroller follows.

Plus every <div> prop.

MessageScrollerJump

Shown only while the reader is away: scrolls to the newest content, follows again and puts focus on the log.

  • labelstring

    Default "Jump to latest"

    The button's accessible name.

  • countnumber

    New rows since the reader scrolled away: shown as "N new" when above 0.

  • childrenReact.ReactNode

    Replaces the arrow (and the count) inside the button.

Plus every <button> prop.

MessageScrollerViewport

The scrolling log: focusable, named, a polite live region unless announce="off".

  • labelstring

    Default "Messages"

    The log's accessible name.

  • announce"additions" | "off"

    Default "additions"

    additions = a polite live region for added rows; off = silent, for a host that announces on its own.

Plus every <div> prop.

Hooks and functions

  • useMessageScroller() => MessageScrollerState

    The state and actions of the nearest MessageScroller: { away, following, toBottom, stick, scrollToItem }.
