Props

Timeline

A vertical list of events on a rail, grouped under headings, with times absolute or relative to now. Compose your own from Timeline.Root, Group, Item, Dot, Content and Time.

  • eventsrequiredreadonly TimelineEvent[]

    The events, in display order; consecutive events with the same group share one heading.

  • labelstring

    Accessible name of an unheaded list.

  • classNamesTimelineClassNames

    A class per part: root, heading, item, dot, title, time, body.

  • renderEvent(event: TimelineEvent) => ReactNode

    Draw one event's content yourself (inside its Timeline.Item, after the dot).

  • classNamestring

    Class for the timeline frame.

  • nowDate

    "Now" for relative times; omit to draw absolute times (SSR-stable).

  • densityTimelineDensity

    Default "default"

    Smaller dots and tighter rows for activity feeds.

Hooks and functions

  • TIMELINE_TONES

    The timeline dot tones, matching the badge tones.

  • TIMELINE_DENSITIES

    The timeline densities: default, or compact for activity feeds.
