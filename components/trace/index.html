Trace

An agent's steps as an ordered list: status icon and text, a title that shimmers while it runs, duration, folded detail and nested steps, with plan, queue and subagent presets. The ai-chat kit's Trace.

Source: @kitora/ai-chat (the single implementation; no other package ships one)

Props

Trace

An ordered list of steps: compose TraceSteps, or pass steps as data.

  • labelstring

    Default "Steps"

    The list's accessible name.

  • stepsreadonly TraceStepData[]

    The steps as data (the preset); each open while it runs until the reader toggles it. Ignored when children is given.

  • statusTraceStatus

    The whole trace's status, written as data-state; derived from steps when omitted.

  • childrenReactNode

    TraceSteps (or TraceSubagents) in order.

Plus every HTML prop.

TraceDetail

A step's folded detail (arguments, output, thinking text): muted, on the indent line.

Plus every HTML prop.

TracePlan

A numbered plan with its progress: waiting steps show their number, the others their status icon.

  • stepsrequiredreadonly TracePlanStep[]

    The plan's steps, in order.

  • titleReactNode

    Default "Plan"

    The heading over the plan.

  • labelstring

    Default "Plan"

    The list's accessible name.

  • progressboolean

    Default true

    Shows "3 of 5 done" by the heading.

  • announceboolean

    Default false

    Reads "Step 3 of 5 done" politely as steps finish; only for a plan outside a transcript (which announces on its own).

  • classNamestring

    Classes for the plan's card.

TraceQueue

A work queue: finished items fold into one "3 done" step, then the running and failed ones, then the queued ones.

  • itemsrequiredreadonly TraceQueueItem[]

    The queue's items, in order.

  • titleReactNode

    Default "Queue"

    The heading over the queue.

  • labelstring

    Default "Queue"

    The list's accessible name.

  • classNamestring

    Classes for the queue's card.

TraceStatusIcon

The status icon (decorative: the status is always also in text). Spins while running, unless the reader asked for less motion.

  • statusrequiredTraceStatus

    The status to draw.

  • classNamestring

    Classes for the icon (its size).

TraceStep

One step: icon, title (shimmering while running), status in text, duration, description, and folded detail or nested steps.

  • statusTraceStatus

    Default "pending"

    Where the step is.

  • titlerequiredReactNode

    What the step does ("Searched the web"); shimmers while running.

  • descriptionReactNode

    One line under the title ("12 results"); wraps.

  • iconReactNode

    Replaces the status icon (decorative).

  • startedAtnumber

    Epoch ms the step started; the duration shows from here to endedAt, ticking while it runs.

  • endedAtnumber

    Epoch ms the step ended; left out while it runs, so the duration ticks.

  • statusLabelstring | false

    Default the `TRACE_STATUS` label

    The status word read after the title by screen readers; false when the title already says it.

  • childrenReactNode

    TraceDetail and / or TraceSteps: makes the step a disclosure.

  • openboolean

    Whether the fold is open (controlled, with onOpenChange); uncontrolled from defaultOpen when omitted.

  • defaultOpenboolean

    Whether the fold starts open when uncontrolled; closed unless the part sets its own default.

  • onOpenChange(open: boolean) => void

    Called when the fold opens or closes, with whether it is open.

Plus every HTML prop.

TraceSteps

Nested steps under a step, on the indent line.

  • labelstring

    The nested list's accessible name, when it needs one of its own.

Plus every HTML prop.

TraceSubagent

A subagent as one step whose detail is its own trace; open while it runs until the reader toggles it.

  • namerequiredReactNode

    The subagent's name ("Explore").

  • avatarReactNode

    Its avatar or icon (decorative); default a bot glyph.

  • stepsrequiredreadonly TraceStepData[]

    Its steps, folded under it as a nested trace.

  • summaryReactNode

    One line under the name ("Explored 12 files · 34s").

  • statusTraceStatus

    Its status; derived from steps when omitted.

  • startedAtnumber

    Epoch ms it started, for its duration.

  • endedAtnumber

    Epoch ms it ended; left out while it runs.

  • classNamestring

    Classes for the step.

  • openboolean

    Whether the fold is open (controlled, with onOpenChange); uncontrolled from defaultOpen when omitted.

  • defaultOpenboolean

    Whether the fold starts open when uncontrolled; closed unless the part sets its own default.

  • onOpenChange(open: boolean) => void

    Called when the fold opens or closes, with whether it is open.

Hooks and functions

  • formatDuration(ms: number) => string

    "4.2s", "1m 3s": short durations for reasoning, tool and step timings.

  • TRACE_STATUS

    The one status map: the word for each status and the tinted badge ToolCallCard draws it in.

  • traceStatusOf(statuses: readonly TraceStatus[]) => TraceStatus

    The status of a list of steps: failed if one failed, running while one runs or some are done and some wait, done when all are.

  • toolStepStatus(status: "pending" | "running" | "result" | "error") => TraceStatus

    A tool call's status as a step status (result is done).
