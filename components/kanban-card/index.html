Props

KanbanCard

The preset: cover, badges, title, description and footer from props, on an <a>, <button> or <div> root.

  • titlerequiredReact.ReactNode

    The task's title.

  • descriptionReact.ReactNode

    Up to two lines of secondary text under the title.

  • badgesKanbanCardBadge[]

    Tinted labels above the title.

  • assigneeKanbanCardAssignee

    The person drawn as an avatar at the footer's end.

  • duestring

    The due date, already formatted ("Mar 4"), in the footer.

  • countKanbanCardCount[]

    Counts in the footer (comments, subtasks), each an icon and a number.

  • hrefstring

    Renders the card as a link.

  • onClickReact.MouseEventHandler<HTMLElement>

    Renders the card as a button (when there is no href).

  • selectedboolean

    Default false

    Whether the card is the selected one: a ring, data-selected and aria-current="true".

  • coverReact.ReactNode

    Full-bleed content above the badges (an image, a colour band).

  • titleAsKanbanCardTitleElement

    Default "h4"

    The title's element.

  • renderuseRender.RenderProp<KanbanCardState>

    Replace the root element (a router link, a custom button); the card's props are merged onto it.

  • refReact.Ref<HTMLElement>

    The root element, for a board that registers the card as a draggable.

  • childrenReact.ReactNode

    Extra content inside the card after the footer.

Plus every HTML prop.

KanbanCardBadges

The row of tinted labels above the title (priority, project).

  • badgesKanbanCardBadge[]

    Draws one tinted label per entry; children are drawn after them.

Plus every <div> prop.

KanbanCardTitle

The card's title, an h4 unless as picks the level that fits the page.

  • asKanbanCardTitleElement

    Default "h4"

    The element: the heading level that fits the page's outline.

Plus every HTML prop.

KanbanCardDescription

Two lines of secondary text under the title.

Plus every <p> prop.

KanbanCardFooter

The bottom row: meta on the start side, the assignee (or any end content) on the end side.

Plus every <div> prop.

KanbanCardMeta

Due date and counts (comments, subtasks) in small muted text; children after them.

  • duestring

    The due date, already formatted ("Mar 4").

  • countKanbanCardCount[]

    The counts after the due date, each an icon and a number.

Plus every <div> prop.

KanbanCardAssigneeAvatar

The assignee's small avatar (image, else initials).

  • assigneerequiredKanbanCardAssignee

    The person to draw.
