A first form

Fields, validation, submit and status with the form engine.

A form from @kitora/form: useForm holds the values, Form wires them to the fields, and each field reads and writes its name. required and the rules validate on blur; Form.Submit runs onSubmit, and Form.Status shows what it returned.

An invite form

Try it: submit it empty, type a name and a broken email, then fix the email and send.

Next: the forms guide goes from this to field arrays and async validation, and the form kit's gallery shows every field.

Relies on: packages/form/src/react/index.ts, packages/form/src/fields/index.ts, src/docs/examples/__tests__/getting-started.test.tsx
