Multi-select drag

Select several items and drag one of them: the selection moves together as one MoveOp, so an undo stack takes one step to put it all back. The preview stacks the items and counts them.

03Wide rows

Click Invoice 1, Shift-click Invoice 3, then drag Invoice 2: the stack is capped at 280 px, so the line under the pointer stays visible. Shift-pressing a row and moving selects; it never starts a drag. From the keyboard: Tab to a row and press X to select it, Shift+X to extend the range, Ctrl+X (Cmd+X on a Mac) to add or remove one; Space or Enter then picks the selection up, arrows move it, Space drops and Escape cancels.
