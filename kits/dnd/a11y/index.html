Keyboard and screen readers

Three keyboard models, chosen per surface: grab-arrow (the default), a Move menu, and Tab-to-target. Every pick-up, move, drop and cancel is announced through one polite live region, in words from a catalog the app can replace; instructions are read when an item gets focus; a list is one tab stop; focus comes back to the moved item.

  • On this page:
  • Grab and arrow
  • Tab to target
  • Move menu
  • One tab stop for a thousand rows
  • Custom and localized text
  • Right to left
  • Tree depth by keyboard
  • Canvas by keyboard
  • Drop on a hidden target
  • Handles and forced colours
  • Keyboard off for one surface
