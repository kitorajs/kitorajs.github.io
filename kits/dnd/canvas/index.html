Free canvas

FreeCanvas positions items anywhere on a bounded surface. The item itself follows the pointer; the modifier pipeline snaps, clamps and locks it, and a drop reports its new position.

01Snap, bounds, axis, magnet and zoom

D47, D79–D82, D87. Drag a note: it snaps to the dotted grid and never leaves the canvas.
