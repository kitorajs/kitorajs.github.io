Print / PDF benefits form

On screen, branches hide. On paper they cannot: the print document expands every Variant case and conditional block with its condition written out, starts each section on a new page, prints entered values read-only and empty answers as blank lines. PDF is the browser's print-to-PDF; no PDF library.
