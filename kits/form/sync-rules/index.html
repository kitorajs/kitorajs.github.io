CRM sync rules

A dense settings form on the declarative API. The workspace number is a conditional mirror of the account-resolved number (app context) with a manual opt-out that restores it; picking or creating a stream back-fills the number through a registered operator over the context's streams list; each row's ••• menu runs named model actions; per-region destinations (a separate mutation) are context that clears regionalIds and forces deletion sync off; a computed count blocks the save until one stream is chosen; the save → snippet page → finish chain is useSubmitPipeline and a failed save shows err.response.errors[0].message form-level through mapErrors. Dashed amber boxes are what the engine cannot express yet.

Sync contacts, companies, deals, tickets and invoices to your destination CRM.

Link your destination account to pick streams from a list, or enter the stream keys by hand.
