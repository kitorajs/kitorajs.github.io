Service booking (real-world)

Booking an onboarding service package, rebuilt on the declarative API. Each Next saves the step; the server's record of completed steps seeds the wizard on reload (<Wizard completed>): it opens at the first unfinished step, completed steps stay checked and reachable, and local edits never move progress back. System rows show their single-sign-on status from app context and open a per-system access modal that writes into that row (RowSubFormDialog). The submit is four phases (useSubmitPipeline); the first Chatterly call fails, and Retry resumes at that row without a second ticket.
