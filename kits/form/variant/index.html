Discriminated-union variants

One <Variant of="…"> swaps the field set per branch. Hidden branches unregister (pruned from the payload); fields outside the variant survive; variants nest. Kills the "scattered conditional logic" anti-pattern.

04Nested variants (payment → card → saved / new)

A variant inside a variant case. Leaving Card clears the whole card subtree in one flush; coming back seeds the branch defaults (saved card preselected), so the required select never shows a phantom error. The inner variant keeps a half-typed new card across saved ↔ new (whenHidden="keep", kept but not submitted). A switch is one flush: the old subtree clears and the new branch seeds before validation runs (switch and fill together with form.batch).Built frominputCreateRelatedButton · formFieldShell · formOptionSelect · formReadOnlyValue · formSheetSelect · formTextChrome · formUsed inaffixalert-dialogapi-key-cardattachmentcomboboxdialogfilter-barfloat-button
