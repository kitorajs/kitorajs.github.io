    05Select a range of cells

    cellSelection makes the table a grid with one tab stop: press and drag, Shift-click or Shift + arrows select a rectangle, Shift+Space a row, Mod+A every cell, Escape clears.     07Paste from a spreadsheet

    Mod+V pastes TSV from Excel, Sheets or Numbers into the editable cells from the focused cell or over the range (tiled when the range is a multiple of the block). Numbers and select labels are coerced like the editors; read-only cells are skipped and onPaste says which.
