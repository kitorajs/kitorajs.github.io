  • W119 · 50 open windows stay smooth (bench)

    Production build, measured 2026-11-25 (task windows:bench, five runs, 48-core Linux at load 10 to 16): dragging a window across the other 49 has a move p95 of 0.20 ms (budget 2 ms) and no long frame at 6× CPU; a press that raises a back window has a p95 of 6.9, 5.0, 5.2, 6.1 and 7.1 ms (5.4 to 7.7 ms in five runs on 2026-11-22) against its budget of 8.5 ms, about 10 % above the worst of those ten; the bundle is within budget (task windows:size). Why the focus budget is not the 4.5 ms of 2026-10-13: that was measured when the site declared far fewer global custom properties. A raise restyles every window above the raised one, and each restyle costs in proportion to the custom properties the window inherits (about 720 today) when the window declares custom properties itself.
