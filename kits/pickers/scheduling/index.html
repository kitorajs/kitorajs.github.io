01Book a meeting

SlotPicker: the host's weekly hours in New York, busy blocks from a fake calendar service, three lengths, a 10-minute buffer, four hours' notice, 60 days ahead and at most six meetings a day. Days with times are marked on the calendar, the rest say why; the times are grouped by part of day in your zone (switch it), with a 12 / 24 h toggle and Next available. The value is two UTC instants.Built frombuttonsegmented-controlCalendar · pickersTimeZonePicker · pickers
