Basics: dates

Values are ISO strings (2026-10-15): no time, no zone, so a day stays that day everywhere. Every calendar is one tab stop; arrows move, PageUp/PageDown change month (Shift for year), Home/End go to the week's ends, the caption drills up to months and years.
