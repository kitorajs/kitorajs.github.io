02Parts

What the kit exports from @kitora/pickers.

CalendarIsoDate · IsoDate[] · DateRangeSingle, multiple or range of days, whole weeks, month, quarter and year grids (one or a range); min, max, blocked days, several months, page behaviour, outside days, month and year drill-up, renderDay.
DateInput · DatePickerIsoDate · IsoDateTime · IsoInstant · IsoZoned · IsoTime · IsoMonth · IsoQuarter · IsoYear · rangesOne picker, eleven date modes (single, multiple, range, week, month, quarter, year and their ranges) plus time and time-range; granularity hour, minute or second for date-times and date-time ranges; a display zone (timeZone, a zone switch or a locked zone in the surface, the zone in the field, a second-zone hint, fixed offsets) with floating, instant or RFC 9557 zoned values; typed segments in every mode or free text with natural language (input="text"), a calendar popover, presets in every mode (rolling, pick and close or stay), compare periods, nights or days, open-ended ranges, one field or two, range ends dragged or moved with Shift+arrows, Apply / Cancel with confirm in every mode, rules as data with reasons (shown on the day, announced, checked on the server by the form kit), async dayData (prices) per month, onChange(value, details); compound parts and a headless useDatePicker.
DateRangePickerDateRangeA DatePicker preset: start and end fields, presets, two months, min and max length, Apply.
MonthPicker · YearPickerYYYY-MM · numberDatePicker presets in month and year mode: month and year grids in a popover.
TimeInput · TimePicker · TimeList · TimeColumnsIsoTime12- or 24-hour by locale or forced, seconds, a list or columns over one headless useTimePanel, steps per unit, blocked times, Now / Noon / End of day presets, a zone's real times on a DST day; TimePicker and DateTimePicker are DatePicker presets.
DateTimePickerIsoDateTimeOne field with date and time segments; calendar and slots in one popover.
TimeZonePickerIANA zone · fixed offsetSearch by city, region, abbreviation or offset; detected zone first; current offset and local time per row; fixed offsets (UTC+05:30) listed like cities; the DatePicker's zone switch.
RecurrenceEditorRRULEDaily, weekly on days, monthly by date or weekday, yearly; ends never, after N, on a date; summary and next dates. Kitora's Select for the frequency by default, or your own control through renderFrequencySelect.
ColorPicker · ColorPickerPopoverhexSaturation/value area, hue and alpha, hex/RGB/HSL inputs, swatches, eyedropper where supported.
NumberInputnumber | nullLocale-aware typing (group and decimal separators, minus variants, currency, percent, unit), commit-on-blur formatting, step without float drift, large step, min and max, wheel when focused; a NumberFormatter adapter.
InlineEdit · createInlineEditany (string preset)Click, Enter or F2 to edit in place; Enter saves, Escape restores, blur saves or cancels; validate, async save with pending, a render slot for any editor.
core—Framework-free date, range, segment, time, RRULE, time-zone, colour and number functions, and the inline-edit reducer; no React, no DOM.
