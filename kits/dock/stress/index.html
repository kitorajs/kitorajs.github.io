Dock kit

A docking layout engine in the class of Golden Layout, FlexLayout and react-mosaic: splits, tab groups and panels as plain data with a pure reducer, built on the repo's own drag engine.

Stress: 200 panels

Two hundred panels in thirty groups, every one kept alive. Dragging, resizing and switching stay smooth because hidden panels skip layout and paint, a sash drag re-renders no panel content, and a change re-renders only the groups it touches.

01200 panels in 30 groups
