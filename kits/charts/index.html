Charts kit

Line, area, bar, combo, pie and donut, radar, radial bar, gauge, funnel, scatter and bubble, sparkline and heatmap charts, and a ranked bars list, on plain SVG, in one style: recessive axes, crosshair tooltips, toggleable legends, a table view, keyboard reading, SVG and PNG export and light and dark palettes. No chart library; the scale and shape maths live in the kit.

02Layers

What the package is made of.

core@kitora/charts/core. Scales and ticks, line / area / bar / arc paths, pie layout, the shared arc (arcGeometry, also drawn by the feedback kit's rings), stacking, regression, keyboard steps, the cartesian and polar engines, the gauge, funnel and bars-list maths (models and layouts as pure data), and the adapters: ChartFormats (createIntlFormats), ChartPalette (createPalette), TextMeasure. No DOM, no React.
dom@kitora/charts/dom. observeWidth (ResizeObserver), localPoint (pointer to plot pixels), elementDirection, and export to SVG and PNG (exportChartSvg, exportChartPng, downloadBlob). No React.
react@kitora/charts/react. Headless: ChartConfigProvider for the adapters, useControllableState, useWidth, useSeriesVisibility, useActiveIndex, useCartesianChart and useCartesianPlot, usePolarChart and usePolarPlot. No styling.
ui@kitora/charts/ui. Chart.* frame parts (with Chart.Export) and ChartFrame; Cartesian.* plot parts and CartesianChart with LineChart, AreaChart, BarChart, ComboChart; Polar.* parts with RadarChart and RadialBarChart; GaugeChart; FunnelChart; BarsList; PieChart and DonutChart; ScatterChart; Sparkline; Heatmap; Legend, TooltipBox, ChartDataTable, ChartStyles.
