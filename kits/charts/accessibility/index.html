Kits

Charts kit

Line, area, bar, combo, pie and donut, radar, radial bar, gauge, funnel, scatter and bubble, sparkline and heatmap charts, and a ranked bars list, on plain SVG, in one style: recessive axes, crosshair tooltips, toggleable legends, a table view, keyboard reading, SVG and PNG export and light and dark palettes. No chart library; the scale and shape maths live in the kit.

Accessibility and themes

Every chart is a labelled group with a hidden summary and data table, a Table view, keyboard reading with live announcements, colour slots that stay distinguishable for common colour-vision deficiencies, and dark steps chosen for the dark surface.
