Charts kit

Line, area, bar, combo, pie and donut, radar, radial bar, gauge, funnel, scatter and bubble, sparkline and heatmap charts, and a ranked bars list, on plain SVG, in one style: recessive axes, crosshair tooltips, toggleable legends, a table view, keyboard reading, SVG and PNG export and light and dark palettes. No chart library; the scale and shape maths live in the kit.

Basics: polar charts

Charts drawn around a centre: a radar compares several metrics across series, a radial bar chart compares values against one full sweep, a gauge shows one value on a dial. The plot reads clockwise from 12 o'clock in either text direction; the legend, table and header follow the page.

01Radar

One row per axis (axis), one closed shape per series. Hover near a spoke, or focus the plot and use the arrow keys, to read every series on it; the legend toggles a series.Built fromChart · chartsChartFrame · chartsPolar · charts

02Radar options

shape polygon or circle, rings, stripes, dots, fill, a fixed domain. A missing value sits at the centre and reads as missing.Built fromChart · chartsChartFrame · chartsPolar · charts

03Radar from the parts

usePolarChart, layoutPolar and the Polar.* parts inside the Chart.* frame: circle rings, no radius labels, the legend under the plot and a custom tooltip.Built fromLegend · chartsUsed inBarsList · chartsCartesianChart · chartsChartFrame · chartsGaugeChart · chartsPieChart · chartsRadarChart · chartsRadialBarChart · chartsScatterChart · charts

04Radial bar

One ring per row against a full sweep (max, here a goal of 100), over faint tracks; 0° to 270° by default so lengths stay comparable. Hover a ring or use the arrow keys.Built fromChart · chartsChartFrame · chartsPolar · charts

05Radial bar options

sort, startAngle / endAngle in degrees, labels={false} with the legend, track={false}, barSize and gap.Built fromChart · chartsChartFrame · chartsPolar · charts

06Gauge

One value between min and max on a 240° dial; segments colour the track and the arc takes the band it ends in. The root is a meter (role="meter"), not a tab stop. Press a CPU button to animate it.Built fromChart · chartsChartFrame · charts

07Gauge and Meter.Ring

A full-sweep gauge beside the feedback kit's Meter.Ring at the same size, thickness and value: one arc drawing (arcGeometry) behind both.Built frommeterChart · chartsChartFrame · chartsUsed inContextUsage · ai-chat
