Basics: funnels and ranked lists

Data where order is the point: a funnel keeps its stages in the order given and shows how many make it from one to the next; a bars list ranks rows largest first.

  • On this page:
  • Funnel
  • Funnel options
  • Bars list
  • Bars list options

01Funnel

label and value per stage, never sorted. Labels show the value and the share of the first stage; the tooltip and the live region add the share of the previous step.Built fromChartFrame · chartsLiveRegion · chartsTooltipBox · charts

02Funnel options

orientation, shape (funnel or bars), gap, labels, percentOf and colorBy. A stage larger than the one before it is drawn as it is.Built fromChartFrame · chartsLiveRegion · chartsTooltipBox · charts

03Bars list

A ranked list in HTML: the name and the value are real text over a bar sized by share of the largest. barsLabel and valueLabel add headers, limit cuts the list and says how many were left out, href makes each row a link.Built fromChart · charts

04Bars list options

onSelect makes rows buttons, color tints each bar, renderLabel adds an icon before the name, max and minBarSize size the bars.Built fromChart · charts
