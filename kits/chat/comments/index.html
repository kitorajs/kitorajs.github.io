Comments on content

Threads anchor to a text range (with its quote kept) or to a whole block. Overlapping ranges each keep their own highlight. Resolve hides a thread from the Open tab; a reply reopens it.

Q3 launch plan

We will launch the new onboarding flow to all workspaces in two waves. Wave one covers teams on the Pro plan; wave two follows a week later once the activation metrics hold steady.

Goals

Success means a 15% lift in day-7 activation and no increase in support tickets about setup. We will measure both in the growth dashboard and review them at the weekly product sync.

Risks

The biggest risk is the data import step: large workspaces can take several minutes, and the current progress bar does not explain what is happening. Engineering is adding resumable imports before wave two.
