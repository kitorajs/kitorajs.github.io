Chat kit

Post in a channel and a teammate reads it, types and answers through the fake realtime adapter. Hover a message to react, reply in a thread, edit or delete; press ↑ in an empty composer to edit your last message; type @ to mention someone; drop or attach a file. Channels with unread messages turn bold, and mentions of you get a red count. Under dir="rtl" the kit mirrors, and in the audit log ← turns to the next page (Audit log has a right-to-left demo).

02Layers

One package, four layers, each its own entry (@kitora/chat/core, /dom, /react, /ui) beside the @kitora/chat barrel.

coreThe model (users, channels, messages, reads, comment threads, activity, audit entries), timeline grouping with day dividers, the light markup parser, reactions and receipts, the comments reducer and highlight math, audit diffs and filters, the store with optimistic sends, the ChatAdapter seam with a fake adapter, and the ChatFormatters seam. No DOM, no React.
domHeadless DOM behaviour: rangeOffsets and selectionIn turn a text selection into comment-anchor offsets; autoGrow sizes a growing textarea. No React, no styling.
reactChatProvider (with formatters) and hooks over the store through useSyncExternalStore: useChatState, useChannelTimeline, useThread, useTyping, useUserLookup, useChannelTitle; useComments and CommentsProvider; useChatFormatters; useControllable; useChatShortcuts (keys as @kitora/keyboard commands). No styling.
uiCompound parts (Chat.Root, Chat.Layout, Chat.Sidebar, Chat.ChannelList, Chat.ChannelRoot, Chat.ChannelHeader, Chat.Messages, Chat.Footer, Chat.Typing, Chat.Composer, Chat.Thread) and the ChatApp / ChannelView presets made from them; MessageItem (drawn through ui's Message, MessageHeader, MessageStatus and MessageActions parts, its day dividers ui's Marker and its files ui's Attachment), Composer, reactions (with the emoji picker from @kitora/pickers), typing and seen; the ChatRenderers seam; CommentableText, CommentableBlock, CommentsRail; ActivityFeed; AuditLogViewer and FieldChanges. The list and layout run on @kitora/sidebar, the phone thread sheet on @kitora/overlay, shortcuts and grid arrows on @kitora/keyboard.
