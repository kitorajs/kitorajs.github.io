Feedback kit

Everything an app says back to its user: toasts with a queue, stacking, swipe, pause, undo and promise states; a notification centre; banners and inline alerts; progress, spinners and skeletons; empty states and result pages. Imperative toast.success(…) plus a provider, on a framework-free core.
