Environments (O45)

<OverlayUiProvider container={…}> moves every popup of a provider into another document or a shadow root. Each box reports where the open popup lives and whether focus came home after the close.

  • On this page:
  • Iframe document
  • Shadow root
  • Portal into a container

01Iframe document

The React tree is portaled into the iframe; the provider's container is the iframe's body, so popups, focus and Escape stay in that document.Built fromEntryView · overlayPresetOverlay · overlayStandardLayout · overlayUsed inaffixalert-dialogapi-key-cardattachmentcomboboxdialogfilter-barfloat-button

02Shadow root

A web-component host: the provider's container is an element inside the shadow root, so popups never land in the light DOM.Built fromEntryView · overlayPresetOverlay · overlayStandardLayout · overlayUsed inaffixalert-dialogapi-key-cardattachmentcomboboxdialogfilter-barfloat-button

03Portal into a container
