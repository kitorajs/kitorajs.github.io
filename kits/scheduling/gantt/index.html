Scheduling kit

Calendars and plans for power users: month, week, day, agenda and resource timeline views with drag to move, resize and create, all-day lanes, side-by-side overlaps, time zones and RRULE recurrence; and a Gantt chart with dependencies, drag bars, zoom levels and a today line. On a framework-free core.

Project plan

A product launch in a Gantt chart. Phases roll up from their tasks, milestones are diamonds, arrows are finish-to-start links and turn red when broken. Drag bars, their ends and the progress triangle; drag a row grip to reorder; hover a bar and drag its end dot onto another bar to link them; click a task to light up its chain.
