Scheduling kit

Calendars and plans for power users: month, week, day, agenda and resource timeline views with drag to move, resize and create, all-day lanes, side-by-side overlaps, time zones and RRULE recurrence; and a Gantt chart with dependencies, drag bars, zoom levels and a today line. On a framework-free core.
