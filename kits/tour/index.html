Tour kit

Press Take the tour. One step waits for you to press the real New project button; the checklist ticks its tasks as you do them, the Reports beacon retires once you've read it, and What's new clears its badge when opened. Everything survives a reload; Reset demo in the sidebar starts over.

02Layers

Each layer is its own entry: @kitora/tour/core, /dom, /react and /ui (the root barrel exports all four). Use only the core, only the hooks with your own markup, or the styled presets.

coreTour reducer (next / back / goTo / skip / complete, conditional steps, progress, resume snapshot), spotlight geometry, checklist reducer and progress, stepper states and click policy (re-exported from feedback), announcement feed, the KeyValueStorage seam. No DOM, no React.
domlocalStorage adapter, target resolver (data-tour or a selector), wait-for-target, live rect observer, scroll into view, reduced motion.
reactuseTour (router adapter, target wait, keyboard with ← and → swapped in right-to-left text, persistence), useChecklist, useStepper (re-exported from feedback), useAnnouncements, useSeenSet, useTargetRect; controlled or not. No markup.
uiTour.* parts and ProductTour on the overlay kit's Popover, TourSpotlight, Beacon, Hotspot, CoachMark, Checklist.* and OnboardingChecklist, Stepper.* and Steps (re-exported from the feedback kit, where they now live; import them from @kitora/feedback in new code), Feed.* and WhatsNew with AnnouncementList; every preset is built only from its parts and takes a class per part.
