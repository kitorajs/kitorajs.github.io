Basics: formulas

A cell starting with = is a formula: + - * / ^, & for text, comparisons, cell references ($ pins a row or column) and ranges, and SUM, AVG, MIN, MAX, COUNT, IF, ROUND, ABS, AND, OR, NOT, CONCAT. While you type one, click a cell to insert its reference.
