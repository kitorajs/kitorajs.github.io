Spreadsheet kit

A spreadsheet grid, separate from the datagrid: every cell is editable and can hold a formula. Click a cell and type, press F2 or double-click to edit, Shift or drag to select ranges, Ctrl-click to add more, drag the square at a selection's corner to fill, drag a column edge to resize (double-click it to fit), and Ctrl+Z to undo any of it.

01A team budget

Twelve months of expense lines with SUM totals both ways, a plan column, variance, an IF status flag and each line's share of the total. Row 1 and column A are frozen. 02Layers

One package, four entries (/core, /dom, /react, /ui), each importing only the layers below it; a test enforces it.

core/addressA1 notation in and out, ranges, the numeric cell key every map uses.
core/valuesThe value union (numbers, text, booleans, errors); the LiteralParser and ValueFormatter seams with defaults (12%, $5, 2026-10-15; any locale and currency).
core/formulaLexer with source positions, precedence parser, evaluator; a FunctionTable seam with SUM, AVG, MIN, MAX, COUNT, IF, ROUND, ABS, AND, OR, NOT, CONCAT built in; reference shifting for copy, fill and sort.
core/engineCells and values; a dependency index (ranges bucketed by column, never expanded); recalculation of only what changed, in topological order, with #CIRC! for cycles.
core/storeSelection with several ranges, editing, formats, a ClipboardCodec seam (TSV default), fill series, sort, freeze, column widths, undo and redo in one transaction per action; a change feed plus toCells / load for controlled cells.
domHeadless behaviour: geometry and windowing maths, pointer tracking, the GridKeymap (Excel keys by default), clipboard wiring, the TextMeasurer seam for auto-fit.
reactUnstyled hooks: useSpreadsheet (uncontrolled or controlled cells), useSheetState, the provider and context the parts share.
uiStyled compound parts: Spreadsheet.Root, .Toolbar (and its groups), .FormulaBar (name box, input), the virtualised .Grid (classNames, data-* state, renderCell slot) and .StatusBar; <Spreadsheet> is built only from them.
