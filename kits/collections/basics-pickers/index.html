Basics: lists and pickers

The smallest working example of each list and picker. Each one takes plain items (id, label) or sections of them. Then a key-value editor and a virtual list, each with its parts. Examples 6 and 7 show how to compose a component from its parts and how to swap an adapter; 13 composes a combobox and 14 shows the shared parts every list is drawn with; 16 is a selectable list of Item rows that hold buttons, and 17 composes it from its parts.
