Basics: menus and toolbars

Menus, menubars and toolbars follow the APG patterns: roving focus over the items, typeahead, and → / ← for submenus. They render the Menu, Menubar and Toolbar parts of @kitora/primitives (the ones the ui dropdown menu, menubar and toolbar run on) under the kit's item-array API.
