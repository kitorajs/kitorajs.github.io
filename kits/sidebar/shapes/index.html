Shapes

One sidebar, every shape: flush, floating and inset; the icon rail, the mini rail with labels and hidden off canvas; pushing the content or covering it; peeking in on hover; the left or the right edge, or both; full height or under a top bar; nested inside a page; mirrored in RTL; and a pinned area that never scrolls.
