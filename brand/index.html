Using the logo

Clear space

One cell on every side: 13 of the mark’s 32 units, about 40 % of its width. For the wordmarks, the same distance measured from the mark.

Minimum size

  • Mark: 16 px; below 24 px use the pixel-tuned 16 px mark (it is the favicon).
  • Horizontal wordmark: 20 px tall.
  • Stacked wordmark: 48 px tall.

Do

  • Keep the parts’ order and colours.
  • Give the mark its clear space.
  • Use the PNG exports where SVG is not accepted.

Don’t

  • Recolour or reorder the parts, rotate or stretch the mark.
  • Add outlines, shadows or gradients, or set “Kitora” in a font.
  • Put the full-colour mark on a busy photo or on the accent blue.
